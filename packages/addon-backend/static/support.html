Support

doc-align is a Google Docs sign-off and alignment add-on for the docalign.app Google Workspace organization. If you're having trouble installing or using it, here's how to get help.

Contact us

Email support@docalign.app with a description of the issue, the Google Doc you were working in (a link is fine), and what you expected to happen instead.

Common issues

  • The add-on doesn't appear in the sidebar — look for the multicolor "G" icon on the right edge of Google Docs, not under Extensions > Add-ons.
  • Signers aren't getting sign-off emails — ask the document owner to confirm the signer's email was entered correctly on the "Add signers" screen.
  • A document shows as "drifted" — this means the document changed after a signer signed off. The signer will be prompted to review the changes and re-sign.
