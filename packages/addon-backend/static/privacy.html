Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-03

This policy explains what doc-align ("the Add-on") accesses and stores when installed in your Google Workspace account within the docalign.app organization.

What we access

The Add-on requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • drive.file — access to the specific Google Doc you open the Add-on in (per-file, granted by you), used to read revision metadata and export document text for drift detection.
  • userinfo.email — your email address, used to identify you as a document owner or signer.

What we store

The Add-on stores only metadata, never document content:

  • Document title, owner, and the Drive revision ID pinned as the sign-off baseline
  • Signer email addresses and their sign-off status (pending, signed, or drifted), plus optional commit messages
  • A history log of sign-off actions (who, when, which revision)

The text or content of your document is never stored. It is only read transiently, in memory, to compute whether a document has drifted from its signed-off revision.

How we use it

Stored data is used solely to run the sign-off workflow: showing status to owners and signers, detecting drift after edits, and sending sign-off and drift notification emails via our transactional email provider (Resend).

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, other than the transactional email provider used solely to deliver the notification emails described above.

Data retention

Sign-off metadata is retained for as long as the document's sign-off record exists. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us below.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@docalign.app.
